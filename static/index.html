How to Play

BitTacToe is a modern take on the classic tic-tac-toe game with real-time multiplayer capabilities.

  • Create a new game or join an existing one
  • Take turns placing X's and O's on the 3x3 grid
  • Get three of your symbols in a row to win
  • Play with friends by sharing your game ID
